Who we are

Quality styling, restoration and care

AutoHub is an automotive customization and vehicle care business based in Borrowdale, Harare. We bring together styling, cosmetic restoration, paintwork and vehicle enhancement to help customers refresh the look and feel of their cars.

Mission: to deliver quality automotive styling, restoration and care services that help customers personalize, maintain and refresh their vehicles with confidence.

Vision: to become a trusted destination in Harare for automotive customization, vehicle appearance enhancement and car care solutions.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who do you work with?

We welcome private vehicle owners and customers seeking appearance upgrades, cosmetic repairs, paintwork, interior refreshes and personalized styling.

How much does a service cost?

Specific scope, materials, timelines and pricing are confirmed during consultation, so the work is matched to your vehicle and goals.

Can I change my car's colour without repainting?

Car wrapping is available for customers who want to change or personalize their car's appearance. Ask us about the options.

Will a body kit fit my car?

Kits are designed according to original vehicle data. Share your vehicle details and we will confirm the right option.

How are parts packed for shipping?

Parts are protected with thickened cartons, pearl cotton and a shockproof design, and can ship by sea, air or express.
